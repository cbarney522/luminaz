# Luminaz Listing Agreement v0.10 (DRAFT - for review by Georgia counsel before launch)

This Listing Agreement (the "Agreement") is between Luminaz Technology LLC, a Georgia limited liability company ("Luminaz"), and the firm identified in the manager account that signs below, on behalf of itself and the fund identified in the listing (together, "Manager"). It takes effect when Manager signs it electronically in the Luminaz manager portal and Luminaz countersigns by approving the listing.

1. WHAT LUMINAZ IS AND IS NOT
1.1 Luminaz operates a technology and discovery platform on which fund managers publish descriptions of private funds and accredited investors may view them and request offering materials directly from the manager.
1.2 Luminaz is not a broker-dealer, investment adviser, placement agent, finder, or fiduciary. Luminaz does not recommend, endorse, or rank any fund; does not solicit, negotiate, or effect any securities transaction; does not process investments or hold investor capital; and does not verify any investor's accredited status.
1.3 Luminaz does not and cannot predict whether or how quickly any fund will attract investors or complete its raise. One fund raising quickly does not mean another will. Manager acknowledges that Luminaz makes no representation about the number, quality, or timing of investor interest.

2. MANAGER'S RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE OFFERING
2.1 Manager is solely responsible for its offering, including compliance with Rule 506(c) of Regulation D, verification of each purchaser's accredited status before any sale, the accuracy and completeness of all offering documents and of the listing content, Form D and state notice filings, and all anti-fraud obligations under federal and state securities laws.
2.2 Manager represents that: (a) the fund is a closed-end pooled investment vehicle with a defined term; (b) the offering relies on Rule 506(c); (c) neither the issuer nor any covered person is subject to a disqualifying event under Rule 506(d), and Manager will notify Luminaz within two business days if that changes; (d) Manager has authority to enter this Agreement and to publish the listing content; and (e) the listing content is true, not misleading, and consistent with the offering documents.
2.3 Manager will not include in any listing content or in any communication referencing Luminaz: guarantees of return or safety, performance projections without a stated basis, comparisons to bank deposits or insured products, statements that Luminaz has approved, endorsed, or recommended the fund, or any statement that an investor's accredited status has been verified by Luminaz.

3. LISTINGS, REVIEW, AND MODIFICATIONS
3.1 Manager enters and maintains its own listing. Luminaz reviews each new listing and each change for completeness, category fit, the representations in Section 2, and plain red flags. After Luminaz approves a listing or a change, it is published only when Manager confirms publication in the portal; nothing about Manager's fund appears to investors without that confirmation. Review is not due diligence, does not verify performance or financial claims, and creates no duty to investors or to Manager.
3.2 Luminaz may decline to publish any listing or change, and may suspend or remove a listing at any time, in its sole judgment, including for a suspected breach of this Agreement, a regulatory inquiry, a complaint, or a disqualifying event. Luminaz will notify Manager and, where practical, allow a reasonable time to cure.
3.3 Changes to a live listing are recorded as numbered modifications. Routine numeric changes that Luminaz designates may take effect immediately; all other changes take effect on approval. The approved listing remains visible while a change is under review.
3.4 Luminaz publishes listings in the order they go live and does not offer ranking, featured placement, or paid positioning.

4. FEES AND PAYMENT
4.1 The listing fee is the monthly rate stated in the manager portal at signing, per fund. Firms designated Founding Managers at first signing keep the Founding rate on every fund they list, for as long as this Agreement remains in effect. Other firms pay the standard rate in effect when each fund goes live.
4.2 Founding Manager terms. Luminaz is a new platform and its investor audience is still being built. In recognition that Manager is among the first forty firms to list before that audience is established, and is therefore accepting the uncertainty of an early platform, Luminaz grants Manager the Founding rate of $400 per month on every fund it lists, locked for the term of this Agreement, and waives the listing fee for the first three months of Manager's first listing. These terms are granted because of when Manager joined. They do not depend on, and will not be adjusted for, the number of investors who view, request, or invest in any listing, and Luminaz makes no representation about that number. Firms after the first forty pay the standard rate stated in the manager portal, with one free month on the firm's first listing. Any free period begins the day the listing is published.
4.3 Invoices issue on the first day of each service month and are due within thirty (30) days. A listing whose invoice is more than ten (10) days past due is paused (removed from the catalog) until paid. Paused months remain payable.
4.4 Manager may close a listing at any time in the portal, including when the fund is fully subscribed. Closing stops future invoices. The final month's fee is prorated by the day through the closing date; any amount already paid beyond that date is credited to Manager's account or refunded on request.
4.5 Luminaz charges no success fee, carried interest, equity, or percentage of capital raised, and will not accept any transaction-based compensation from Manager.
4.6 Luminaz may change standard rates on sixty (60) days' notice. Locked Founding rates do not change.

5. INVESTOR CONTACT AND DATA
5.1 An investor's name and email reach Manager only when the investor requests materials. Shortlist and watch activity is shown to Manager only as counts.
5.2 Manager will use investor contact information solely to respond to that investor's request about the listed fund, will honor opt-outs, and will not sell, rent, or share it. Manager is responsible for its own privacy and anti-spam compliance.
5.3 Luminaz does not sell information about investors or managers. Luminaz's handling of data is described in its Privacy Policy, which forms part of this Agreement.

6. CONTENT LICENSE, TRADEMARKS, AND PUBLICITY
6.1 Manager owns its listing content and grants Luminaz a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to display, reproduce, and technically adapt it on the platform and in platform notifications, for the term of the listing and for records thereafter.
6.2 Manager may state that its fund is "listed on Luminaz" and may use the listing link. Manager will not use the Luminaz name or marks in any other way, and will not imply Luminaz endorsement, without written consent.
6.3 Luminaz may identify Manager as a listed firm (name and fund name) in platform materials unless Manager opts out in writing.

9. TERM AND TERMINATION
9.1 This Agreement continues while Manager has any listing open and for twelve (12) months after the last listing closes, so that Sections 5 through 8 and 10 continue to apply. Sections 2.2(c), 5, 6.1 (as to records), 7, 8, and 10 survive termination.
9.2 Either party may terminate on written notice. Luminaz may terminate immediately for breach, a disqualifying event, or a regulatory reason.

10. DISPUTES
10.1 The parties will first try in good faith to resolve any dispute by direct discussion within thirty (30) days of written notice.
10.2 If that fails, the parties will submit the dispute to non-binding mediation in Cobb County, Georgia, or by video, with a mutually acceptable mediator, before either brings any claim. Each party bears its own attorneys' fees and costs and one half of the mediator's fee.
10.3 Any claim not resolved by mediation will be brought in the state or federal courts located in Cobb County, Georgia, and the parties consent to that jurisdiction and venue. Georgia law governs, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules.
10.4 Any claim must be brought within one (1) year after it arises. THE PARTIES WAIVE TRIAL BY JURY.
