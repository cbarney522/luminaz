# Luminaz Privacy Policy v0.9 (DRAFT - for review by Georgia counsel)

Luminaz Technology LLC ("Luminaz") operates luminaztechnology.com and the Luminaz investor and fund manager portals.

What we collect
- Investors: name, email, password (stored hashed), the accreditation basis you select and when you attested to it, your shortlist and watch marks, your material requests and any note you attach, the funds you view, and standard technical logs (IP address, browser, times).
- Accreditation calculator: only your result (accredited or not, and the test met) and your email. The dollar figures you enter are used to compute the result and are not stored.
- Fund managers: name, email, firm details you enter, principals you list, listing content, signature records (typed name, title, time, IP address), invoices and payment status, and technical logs.
- Everyone: emails we send you and whether they bounce.

How we use it
- To run the platform: accounts, listings, requests, invoices, security, and support.
- To share an investor's name, email, attested basis, and note with a fund manager when, and only when, that investor requests that manager's materials.
- To show managers anonymous counts of views, shortlists, and watches.
- To send the emails you agreed to when you gave us your address: service notices (which continue while you have an account) and educational emails and platform updates (which you can stop at any time). We keep the date and wording of your consent. Every educational or marketing email carries an unsubscribe link and a one-click unsubscribe header; using either stops those emails at once and adds your address to a do-not-email list that we keep so we do not contact you again. Fund managers we contact about listing receive the same opt-out.
- To check fund managers and their principals against public regulatory and sanctions sources under Rule 506(d).
- To keep the records the law and our agreements require.

What we do not do
- Luminaz does not sell, rent, or trade information about investors or fund managers, to anyone, in any form.
- Luminaz does not share investor identities with managers except on the investor's own request for materials.
- Luminaz does not use your data to rank or recommend funds.

Service providers. We use providers for hosting, email delivery, and (if adopted) payment processing. They process data only on our instructions. We will list them on request.

Retention. Account data is kept while your account is open and for the period our agreements and the law require afterward (generally up to seven years for signature, invoice, and vetting records). Calculator results are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Your choices. You can update your information in your account, unsubscribe from educational emails, close your account, or ask us to delete your data (subject to records we must keep) by writing to support@luminaztechnology.com.

Security. Passwords are hashed, admin access requires two-step sign-in, data is backed up off-server, and access is logged. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you suspect a problem.

Children. Luminaz is for adults who are accredited investors or fund managers. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes. We will post changes here and, for material changes, email account holders.

Contact. Luminaz Technology LLC, Georgia, USA. support@luminaztechnology.com.
