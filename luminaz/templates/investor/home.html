Many people who qualify don't know it

The SEC's accredited investor thresholds were set in 1982 and have never been adjusted for inflation. Two minutes with the calculator tells you where you stand. We keep your result and your email, never your figures.

What is checked before a fund is listed

A signed listing agreement, a Rule 506(d) bad-actor check with a determination recorded by a person, a review of the listing text, core fund documents on file, and the manager's own publish step. We describe funds; we never recommend them.

No ranking, no paid placement

Funds appear in the order they were listed. Your name reaches a manager only when you ask for their materials. Luminaz does not sell your information.
