Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use Luminaz. This page says what you can change, what we have done, and what we have not tested yet.

Make the page easier to read

These settings are saved on this device only. We do not send them anywhere.

Using Luminaz with a screen reader or a keyboard

  • The first link on every page is "Skip to main content". Press Tab once, then Enter, to jump past the menu.
  • Every form field has a label, and every button says what it does.
  • Headings are used in order, so you can move between them with your screen reader's heading keys.
  • Everything can be done with the keyboard: Tab to move, Enter or Space to press a button.
  • Your browser's own zoom (Ctrl and +, or Command and +) also works, up to 200 percent.

What we have done, and what we have not

  • Done: labels on every field and button, readable button text, links that are underlined inside text, and an automated check of the main pages against the WCAG 2.1 A and AA guidelines.
  • Not yet: a test by people who use screen readers every day. We have not had that done yet, so there may still be things that are hard to use.

Tell us what is hard to use

If something on Luminaz is hard for you to use, write to {{ setting('support_email') }} and say which page and what happened. A person will read it, and we will fix what we can or help you another way.
