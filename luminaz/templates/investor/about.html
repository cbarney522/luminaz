About Luminaz

One list of private funds, in plain order, with the checks written down.

Why we built it

Diane spent years as a financial adviser and then eighteen years managing Department of Defense contracts. She is an accredited investor who has invested in private funds and pre-IPO companies, and she kept meeting people who qualified and had no idea. Luminaz is the place she wished existed: a listing site where accredited investors can find funds they like, free, and ask the manager directly.

Who runs it

{{ company }} is a Georgia company owned by its two founders, Diane and her husband Cliff, who builds and runs the technology. The founders are accredited investors and may invest in listed funds on the same terms as anyone; a listing says so when they have, and that gives the fund no advantage on the site.

What we will never do

  • Rank, feature or recommend a fund. Listings appear in the order they were received.
  • Take a fee tied to any investment. Fund managers pay a flat monthly listing fee.
  • Touch investor money, pass documents through Luminaz, or sit between an investor and a manager.
  • Tell anyone a fund is good, safe or approved.
  • Verify or certify accreditation. The fund manager verifies under Rule 506(c).
  • Sell data about investors or managers.

What is checked before a fund is listed

  • Reliance on Rule 506(c) and one of the Luminaz categories.
  • A Rule 506(d) bad-actor check, with a determination recorded by a person.
  • Core documents on file: private placement memorandum, operating agreement, subscription documents. Seen by Luminaz only.
  • The listing text, for claims that need proof. No projected returns anywhere.
  • A signed listing agreement, Luminaz's approval, and the manager's own publish step.
