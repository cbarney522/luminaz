What Luminaz is, and is not

{{ company }} operates a listing platform. Luminaz is not a broker-dealer, investment adviser or finder. It does not offer, sell or recommend securities, does not solicit investors on any manager's behalf, does not handle investor money, does not negotiate or process any investment, and receives no fee tied to any investment. Fund managers pay a flat monthly listing fee.

Luminaz does not verify or certify anyone's accredited investor status. Each fund manager is solely responsible for verifying accredited status under Rule 506(c) before accepting an investment, and for its own offering, documents and communications. Listings appear in the order received; no fund is endorsed, approved, ranked or recommended. Private funds involve risk, including loss of the entire investment. Read the offering documents and consult your own advisers.
