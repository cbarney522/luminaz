What you get

  • A listing you write and update yourself, numbered and tracked like a contract.
  • Investor views, anonymous shortlist and watch counts.
  • Every request for materials in a private pipeline, with reminders.
  • A promotion kit for your own list, and a link investors can trust.

What we check

  • Reliance on Rule 506(c) and one of the Luminaz categories.
  • The Rule 506(d) bad-actor check, with a determination recorded by a person.
  • Core documents on file: PPM, operating agreement, subscription documents. Seen by Luminaz only.
  • Your listing text, for claims that need proof. No projected returns anywhere.

Every listing is reviewed before investors see it, and so is every change to its name, category, strategy or key terms. A change to the target raise or minimum investment goes live at once and is logged.
